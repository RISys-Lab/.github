# .github# RISys Lab

**Robust Intelligent Systems Lab**

[Research website](https://muzammal-naseer.com/) | [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/RISys-Lab) | [All repositories](https://github.com/orgs/RISys-Lab/repositories)

We advance multimodal learning and develop foundation models for understanding and reasoning about the physical world. Our research emphasizes AI safety, with the goal of making these systems more reliable and trustworthy.

We share research code, model checkpoints, datasets, and benchmarks to support reproducible evaluation and further research.

## Research directions

- **Multimodal learning and foundation models:** Learning representations that connect information across modalities and support understanding and reasoning.
- **Grounded understanding and reasoning:** Connecting model predictions to visual evidence, spatial relationships, and temporal context.
- **AI safety, reliability, and evaluation:** Investigating model capabilities and limitations, developing benchmarks, and improving reliability in challenging and safety-critical settings.
